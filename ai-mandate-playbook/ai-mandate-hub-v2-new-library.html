The mandate has arrived.

Somewhere in the past 18 months, the question changed. It's no longer "should we be using AI?" It's "why aren't we further along?" Controllers and finance leaders across every sector are receiving the same directive from their CFOs and boards: move faster on AI. The expectations are high. The margin for error is zero.

"You need AI that drives your team forward. Not AI you'll rip out in 18 months."

The problem isn't ambition. It's sequence.

Most AI mandates fail not because teams weren't willing to try, but because they started in the wrong place. They bought tools before their data was clean. They ran pilots before they had baselines. They automated workflows that weren't documented. The technology worked fine. The foundation wasn't there to support it.

There is a right order.

Get the data right first. Then calibrate your risk honestly — not every workflow tolerates the same error rate. Then get real proof: baselines, benchmarks, parallel runs. Then experiment widely in a sandbox where mistakes are cheap. Then, and only then, implement carefully in the GL where they're not.

What this playbook is.

This is not a collection of articles to read. It's a set of tools, templates, and frameworks to use — built with and for the Controllers and accounting leaders who are actually doing this work. Every resource here is designed to help you make a decision, communicate clearly, or act with confidence.
